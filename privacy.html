Privacy Policy for Redeo

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Redeo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no servers operated by the developer, and no analytics or advertising. Everything you write stays on your device.

What Redeo stores, and where

A Redeo card is a question and an answer, both typed by you. Redeo keeps three kinds of data, all in a single database in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your cards: the question and answer text, and when each card is next due;
  • a history of your reviews: the date, the score and the resulting schedule;
  • your settings, such as theme, reminders and score thresholds.

This storage is not readable by other apps. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

How answers are graded

When you write an answer from memory, Redeo compares it with the answer you saved using two methods that both run entirely on your device: a word comparison, and a small language model bundled inside the app. Your answers are never sent to a server for grading, or for any other purpose.

Network access

Redeo does not declare the INTERNET permission, so the app itself cannot connect to the internet. The permissions it does declare are:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, for the optional once-a-day study reminder;
  • com.android.vending.BILLING, so the Google Play Store app can handle a subscription purchase;
  • WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which are added automatically by Android's WorkManager library, used to schedule the reminder. None of these allow Redeo to send data.

Subscriptions

Redeo Pro is sold through Google Play. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play: Redeo never sees your payment details, and only learns from the Play Store app on your device whether a subscription is active. Google processes purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include Redeo's database in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so your cards can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is managed by Android and Google. The developer has no access to it. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. The developer has no access to your cards, your answers, or your review history.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting a card inside the app removes the card from your device.
  • Uninstalling Redeo removes its database and everything in it from your device.

Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no copy for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Children

Redeo is a general-purpose study tool. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them, or from anyone else.
